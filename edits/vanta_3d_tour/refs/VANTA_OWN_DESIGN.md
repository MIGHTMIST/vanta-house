## IMPORTANT FORMAT NOTE
Every vanta_vr_* file is 2560 x 15360 = SIX stacked 2560x2560 cubemap faces (a 360 VR render, hence "vr"), NOT a normal photo. Face i = rows i*2560..(i+1)*2560. Observed order in the contact sheets (3x2 grid reading left-right, top-bottom): faces 0,1,2 = the three wall views (front / side / side), 3 = fourth wall view, 4 = ceiling (up), 5 = floor (down). Faces 0-3 are the horizontal walls (face 3 is the 4th wall in most files), so one render = the whole room. A 3D artist can drop the strip into a cubemap/skybox to look around each room, or crop faces for reference.
Do NOT use the raw strips as ad images. Crop single wall faces.
