## Used in all four
- **Fonts:** Anton for keywords, numbers and cards. Inter Bold for captions, white with a soft shadow; the spoken keyword turns gold (#D9B36C). Nothing else.
- **Placement:** no text over a face. Cards go above the head, beside John, or behind him (John cut out with a mask so the card sits behind him).
- **Motion:** every graphic eases in and out (0.35 s), with a sound on the way in and on the way out (pop_soft / whoosh_cinematic_5 / whoosh_medium_3).
- **Look:** warm, clean grade; slow push-ins only.
- **Sound:**
  - John's voice cleaned (high-pass, light de-noise, gentle compressor), with room_tone_office_v2 under any cut gap.
  - Music about 18–20 dB under his voice.
  - One mix, −14 LUFS, true peak −1.5.
- **CTA end card:**
  - Text, word for word: "Send us the word VISIT on WhatsApp, and we'll send you the floor plans and prices for free. See it first, then pay in parts."
  - **0741 131 420** in Anton.
- **Credit:** Oculus sounds are used (whooshes, pop, paper, clock, room tone, site ambience, typing, impact, reverse swell). "Sounds: Oculus Audio Pack 01 by Oculus VR, LLC, CC BY 4.0" goes small on the end card and in the post text. The Mixkit and FreePD music needs no credit.
- **Cut everywhere:**
  - 8:20–8:56: "70 units sold" and "clients are Chinese".
  - 4:06: the "competitive pricing … rental income potential" sentence.
  - "sustainable investment returns" (2:19).
  - "including Kololo" (ads only).
  - Nothing on screen says sold %, yield, rent, returns, "on time", "safe", "fully finished", "discount", "cheap", "China Speed".
- **Captions:** "10 years" (not "two"); "Vanta House" where he says "Van House"; "Kampala" and "Naguru" spelled right.
